# Guide Index

Use this page to choose the smallest guide that answers your question. Difficulty is relative: **Foundation** introduces the subject, **Applied** expects basic familiarity, and **Advanced** focuses on scale, trade-offs, or production failure.

Start with [Start Here](START-HERE.md) if you want a complete study sequence.

### Fanout Public Roadmap Coverage

The repository uses the publicly visible [Fanout roadmap catalogue](https://fanout.sh/roadmaps) and public curriculum titles as a coverage checklist. Explanations, examples, questions, and exercises in this repository are original; paid lesson text is not reproduced.

| Fanout area | Repository coverage |
|---|---|
| AI Engineering | [AI Engineering Design](ai-engineering-design.md) for the model-call stack, embeddings/retrieval, LLM internals, optimizations, reasoning/verification, MCP/agents, and evaluation; [AI in .NET](ai-dotnet.md) for implementation |
| Inference Engineering | [Inference Engineering](inference-engineering.md) for all public curriculum spaces from one-token execution through engine research and capstone practice |
| AI Research | [AI Mathematics & Research Workbook](ai-math-research-workbook.md) for derivation/implementation/reproduction practice and [AI System Design](ai-system-design.md) for transformers, ML pipelines, evaluation, reinforcement learning, MCP, and production architectures |
| ML Mathematics and notation | [AI Mathematics & Research Workbook](ai-math-research-workbook.md) for notation, linear algebra, calculus/backprop, probability/information theory, optimization, tensor shapes, and worked answer keys |
| System Design | [System Design Interview Guide](system-design-interview-guide.md), [Fundamentals](system-design-fundamentals-extended.md), [HLD](hld.md), and [Extended Designs](system-design-extended.md) |
| Interactive labs and deliberate practice | [Hands-On Engineering Labs](HANDS-ON-LABS.md) plus the topic-wise workbooks inside the AI and inference guides |
| Open robotics | Distributed-systems, messaging, observability, safety, and AI foundations are covered here; hardware control, ROS 2, kinematics, planning, and simulation remain a separate specialization rather than being presented as complete coverage |
